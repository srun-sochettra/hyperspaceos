# Community expectations

Keep discussions respectful, specific and focused on the work. Critique ideas and implementation choices without attacking people. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Use non-sensitive examples in reports. Do not post another person's documents, camera images or credentials. Respect consent and privacy when sharing screenshots or recordings.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. No confidential reporting channel or response timetable is promised by this document. Use an available private contact route for sensitive concerns; otherwise request one without disclosing private details.
